2.What Tracercoin is

Tracercoin (TFX) is an independent, community proof-of-work blockchain secured by Scrypt mining. TFX is a utility coin obtained by mining — there is no token sale, pre-sale, or public offering, and buying TFX from Tracercoin is not part of the Services. The chain can also record permanent, verifiable proofs called fingerprints. TFX is intended for mining, transacting, and recording proofs — not as an investment.
